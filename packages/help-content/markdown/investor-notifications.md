## How you are notified

CashSouk can reach you in two places:

- **In the portal** — open the bell in the header, or go to **Notifications**
- **By email** — the same update sent to the email on your account

Most alerts appear in both places. A password-change alert always does, so you know if someone changed your sign-in details.

You can manage marketing emails from **Account**. Invitation emails are sent separately when someone is asked to join your organisation.

## Your account

- **Onboarding approved** — your organisation is ready to invest
- **Onboarding rejected** — your application was not approved
- **Password changed** — your account password was updated
- **Director or shareholder action required** — someone in your company still needs to complete onboarding

## Your investments

- **Funding failed** — a note you reserved did not complete funding, so the reserved amount was released
- **Note active** — a note you funded is now active
- **Repayment recorded** — a repayment was recorded on a note
- **Settlement posted** — a settlement has been posted to your organisation
- **Note in arrears** — a note you invested in is overdue
- **Note defaulted** — a note you invested in was marked in default
- **Withdrawal submitted to trustee** — a withdrawal instruction has been sent to the trustee

## Products and announcements

- **New product alert** — CashSouk is highlighting a new investment opportunity
- **System announcement** — a general platform update or notice

If you no longer want marketing emails, turn them off in **Account**.
