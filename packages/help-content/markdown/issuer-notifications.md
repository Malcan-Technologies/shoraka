## How you are notified

CashSouk can reach you in two places:

- **In the portal** — open the bell in the header, or go to **Notifications**
- **By email** — the same update sent to the email on your account

Most alerts appear in both places. A password-change alert always does, so you know if someone changed your sign-in details.

Invitation emails, document-signing links, and one-time codes are sent separately from this list.

## Your account

- **Onboarding approved** — your organisation is ready to use the issuer portal
- **Onboarding rejected** — your application was not approved
- **Password changed** — your account password was updated

## Your financing applications

- **Amendments requested** — CashSouk asked you to update your application
- **Acceptance documents need updates** — papers after an offer still need a change
- **Application rejected** — the application was not approved
- **Facility offer sent** — a facility offer is ready for you to review
- **Invoice offer sent** — an invoice offer is ready for you to review
- **Offer retracted or reset** — a sent offer was pulled back
- **Offer expiry reminder** — a deadline to accept or sign is coming up
- **Offer expired** — the offer deadline has passed
- **Application resubmitted** — we received your updated application
- **Application withdrawn** — the application was withdrawn
- **Application completed** — the application has finished
- **Director or shareholder action required** — someone in your company still needs to complete onboarding
- **Upfront facility fee required** — a facility fee must be paid before invoice financing can start
- **Upfront facility fee paid** — that fee has been received
- **Outstanding late charges** — late payment charges are due after a settlement
- **Late payment charges received** — those charges have been paid

## Your notes

- **Note published** — your note is live for funding
- **Funding succeeded** — funding closed successfully
- **Funding failed** — the note did not reach the minimum
- **Note active** — the note is active after funding
- **Note repaid** — the note has been fully repaid
- **Note in arrears** — a repayment is overdue
- **Note defaulted** — the note was marked in default
- **Withdrawal submitted to trustee** — a withdrawal instruction has been sent to the trustee

## Announcements

CashSouk may also send a general platform announcement. Those messages appear in the same bell and inbox.
